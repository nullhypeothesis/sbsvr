Side-by-Side VR for AR Glasses

Watch VR180, 360° and 3D videos on your XREAL One or One Pro glasses, from your Mac.

  • Plays side-by-side and top-bottom 3D, 180° and 360° video, and fisheye lenses up to 220°, detected automatically.
  • Opens files from Finder, links you paste, or videos straight from websites in Safari with the included extension, including sites you’re signed in to.
  • Center view brings what you’re watching to eye level; eye alignment fixes badly lined-up videos.
  • One click puts the video on the glasses, in 3D.
